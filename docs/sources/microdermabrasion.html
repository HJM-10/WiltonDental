Professional skin treatments

Treatments designed to refresh and renew.

Explore our microdermabrasion and microneedling services and discover which approach may be more suitable for your skin goals.

✨ Exfoliate · Smooth · Refresh

Microdermabrasion

A non-invasive exfoliating treatment that gently removes dead surface skin cells, helping to reveal a brighter and smoother-looking complexion.

Microdermabrasion may help to:

  • Improve the appearance of dull or tired-looking skin
  • Refine rough or uneven surface texture
  • Reduce surface congestion and unclog pores
  • Enhance the absorption of suitable skincare products
  • Support a fresher and more polished appearance

Treatment suitability and frequency depend on your skin type, skin condition and treatment goals.

◌ Stimulate · Renew · Restore

Microneedling

A skin-renewal procedure that creates controlled microchannels in the skin to encourage its natural repair process and support collagen production.

Microneedling may help improve:

  • The appearance of fine lines and wrinkles
  • Uneven skin texture and selected acne scarring
  • Enlarged-looking pores and general skin quality
  • Skin firmness and overall smoothness
  • The appearance of uneven tone

Microneedling is not suitable for everyone. A consultation is important to assess your skin, medical history and treatment suitability.
